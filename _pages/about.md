I am a second-year PhD candidate in environmental economics at Paris-Saclay Applied Economics (PSAE), a research unit of the French National Research Institute for Agriculture and the Environment (INRAE) and Université Paris-Saclay. I am co-supervised by Guy Meunier (PSAE) and Thierry Brunelle (CIRED). 

My research interests include environmental and agricultural economics, land-use allocation, and the design of climate and bioenergy policies. I use theoretical economic models and numerical simulations to study how land-use decisions can support the energy transition and environmental goals.
