I am a second-year PhD candidate in environmental economics at Paris-Saclay Applied Economics (PSAE), a research unit of INRAE and AgroParisTech within Université Paris-Saclay. I am co-supervised by Guy Meunier (PSAE) and Thierry Brunelle (CIRED). 

My research interests include environmental and agricultural economics, land-use allocation, and the design of climate and bioenergy policies. I use theoretical economic models and numerical simulations to study trade-offs between food production, bioenergy, and carbon sequestration.
